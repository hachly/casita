# Security

Report vulnerabilities privately through GitHub: on this repository's **Security** tab, choose **Report a vulnerability**. Please don't open a public issue, and keep the details private until a fix is on the App Store.

We fix problems on a best-effort basis and credit reporters who want it.

What counts most: anything that reveals how many spaces exist or which one is the top space, lets someone guess codes faster than the entry screen allows or away from the iPhone, reads a space's contents without its code, or sends anything from a space off the iPhone.
